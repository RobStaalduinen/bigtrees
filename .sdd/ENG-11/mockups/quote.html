Scope of work

Remove two silver maples from the rear yard's north fence line, both with heavy deadwood and a split union over the pool deck. Reduce the rear-corner Norway maple, deadwood and crown clean the front-walk bur oak, trim the east cedar hedge, and grind both maple stumps below grade.

What's included
  • Sectional removal of two silver maples, rigged down over the pool deck
  • Crown reduction of the rear-corner Norway maple, roughly 20 per cent
  • Deadwood removal and crown clean of the front-walk bur oak
  • Cedar hedge trimmed to a level line along the east property edge
  • Stump grinding on both maples to 8 in. below grade, grindings raked level
  • All brush chipped and hauled off site, logs cut to 16 in. on request, and a full cleanup before we leave
Not included
  • Topsoil, sod or replanting over the ground stumps
  • Removal of surface roots or root flare beyond the stump collar
  • Repair of fence, pool deck or hardscape damage that predates the work
  • Town of Loyalist permit fees, should the front-yard oak require one

Before we start

A few things that make the day go smoothly.

  1. You don't need to be home. We've done this thousands of times. Don't take a day off for it — we don't collect payment on the day, and we don't expect it same-day.
  2. Clear the route to the tree. The driveway, path and yard need to be open enough for us to carry branches out.
  3. Move anything you'd hate to lose. Statues, pots, birdfeeders, lights, furniture, barbecues. Tell us about anything you couldn't move, and about any sprinkler lines or septic tanks, before we start.
  4. Let your neighbours know. Pass along our number if they have questions — have them reference your name and address.
  5. Mow beforehand if you can. We can't do a thorough cleanup of small branches in long grass.
  6. Schedules move. The work is hard on bodies and equipment, and weather decides a lot of it. Breakdowns and delays happen, we often run a little behind, and we'll keep you updated as the week goes.
  7. Emergencies come first. If an existing customer calls in storm damage or an unexpected hazard, we'll revise the schedule to handle it.
  8. What causes a delay. Unsafe weather — rain, snow, wind, storms. Equipment down for repair. An injured or ill crew member leaving us shorthanded. We'll reschedule when any of these make the work unsafe.
Questions about any of this before the crew arrives? Call 1-877-542-5551 and ask for Tom.
